Dive plan

Bathyscaphe

A relaxed trivia trip in a very adaptable little sub. Steer through the opening marked with the right answer to keep going.

  • Get 10 in a row and you warp to a new world: the ocean, a volcano, deep space, the ice caverns.
  • No game over. A wrong answer just comes back later for another try.
  • Four question types: multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank and type the answer.
  • Hints cross out wrong answers. You earn one for every 3 in a row.
